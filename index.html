About me

Exploring engineering challenges across neurotechnology and rehabilitation.

I'm a biomedical engineer interested in neuroengineering, rehabilitation and medical technologies. I enjoy approaching problems from different angles, combining software, data analysis and hands-on engineering.

My academic projects span biomedical signal analysis, medical imaging, deep learning, IoT-based health monitoring and UR robot programming. In research, I've worked on machine-learning decision support for urease-powered PLGA nanomotor optimization.

I'm particularly drawn to multidisciplinary teams developing technology that can translate from research into real-world rehabilitation and healthcare.
